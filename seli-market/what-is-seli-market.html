What is Seli Market?

Last updated September 2026

Seli Market is a video-first marketplace built for Nigeria. Sellers showcase products with short video clips and photos, buyers browse and shop, and every successful order is delivered by a verified Seli Pilot.

What makes Market different is trust. Your payment is held in escrow and, if no dispute is raised, is automatically released to the seller 24 hours after delivery confirmation — so the money is never released to the seller before you receive what you paid for.

Who is Seli Market for?

  • Buyers — someone who wants to shop securely for fashion, electronics, homeware and handmade goods from verified sellers.
  • Sellers / merchants — individuals and small businesses that want a storefront with built-in payment protection and tracked delivery.
  • Creators — anyone who can show a product honestly and clearly on video.

How it works at a glance

  1. Browse or search the marketplace and open a product you like.
  2. Place your order and pay — your money goes straight into escrow, not to the seller.
  3. A verified Seli Pilot picks up your item and delivers it to you, with live tracking.
  4. Confirm the item matches — if no dispute is raised, escrow auto-releases payment to the seller 24 hours after delivery.

Products you can buy

Market works best for physical products that fit in a Pilot's delivery bag: clothing, accessories, gadgets, phones, electronics, homeware, cosmetics, books and handcrafted goods.

Some items are not allowed on the platform. See Prohibited and Restricted Items for the list.

Good to know

Seli Pilot, Seli Market and Shemi AI are part of the same Seli platform and share one account. Sign in once and you can buy, sell, deliver and generate listings with the same profile.
