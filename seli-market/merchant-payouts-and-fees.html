Merchant Payouts and Fees

Last updated September 2026

Selling on Seli Market is free to start — you only pay a fee when you make a sale. Here's how merchant earnings work.

The seller fee

Seli charges a 5.5% seller fee on every completed order, deducted from your payout. There are no listing fees, no monthly store fees and no hidden charges.

Example: you sell an item for ₦10,000. The 5.5% fee (₦550) and any delivery fee choose to share are deducted, and the rest is yours.

When you get paid

Escrow auto-releases to your Seli wallet 24 hours after delivery confirmation if the buyer raises no dispute — you never wait for money at the point of sale, and you never chase the buyer to confirm. Most merchants receive their bank payout within 1–2 business days of release.

How to get paid

  1. Add a bank account
    Enter a bank account in your name in Store → Settings → Payout.

  2. Confirm the account
    We deposit a small test amount you must confirm — this proves the account is yours.

  3. Track earnings
    Open Earnings to see pending, available and paid balances per order.

  4. Withdraw
    Tap 'Withdraw' to send available funds to your bank.

Holds and deductions

If a buyer raises a claim that could result in a refund, we may place the affected amount on hold while it's reviewed. On a full refund, the fee for that order is also returned to you.

No fee refunds to buyers?

Buyers never pay the seller fee — it's shown to you only. Buyers see the price plus any delivery fee at checkout.
