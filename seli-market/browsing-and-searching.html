Browsing and Searching

Last updated September 2026

You don't need an account to browse the Seli marketplace - but you do need one to buy, to chat with a seller, or to save products to your favourites.

Finding products

  1. Open the app
    Open the Seli app and tap the Market tab. The home feed shows recommended products based on what you watch and search for.

  2. Search
    Use the search box at the top to look for a product, brand, or keyword. Try 'sneakers', 'power bank', or 'leather bag'.

  3. Filter
    Narrow results by category, price range, and seller verification. You can also filter to only show items that ship with a delivery fee you accept.

  4. Watch and save
    Products play as short videos in the feed. Tap the video to open the full listing, or save it to Favourites for later.

Understanding product pages

  • Video - the seller's product video plays automatically. Watch it in full before you buy.
  • Verified badge - a checkmark next to the seller name means they completed extra identity checks.
  • Escrow protection - every listing shows that payment is protected until delivery.
  • Shop section - the seller's other listings so you can bundle items and save on delivery.

Favourites

Tap the bookmark icon on any product to save it to Favourites. Your favourites are private to you and are stored on your account, so you can view them from any device.

Tip - compare similar items

Sellers often list the same item at different prices. Open two or three listings for the same product and compare condition, delivery fee and the seller's rating before you commit.
