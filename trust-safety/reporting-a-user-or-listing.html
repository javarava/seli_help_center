Reporting a User or Listing

Last updated September 2026

Seli is a community marketplace. If you see something wrong, one report keeps the platform safer for everyone.

What you can report

  • A listing showing a prohibited or misdescribed item.
  • A seller who behaves badly, spams, or misrepresents products.
  • A buyer who abandons orders or harasses you.
  • A Pilot violation — unsafe riding, rude behaviour.
  • A verified account that is impersonating you.
  • Scams, phishing, or off-platform payment requests.

How to report

  1. Open the listing or profile
    Go to the relevant product, store, or user.

  2. Tap the three-dot menu
    Choose 'Report'.

  3. Pick a reason
    Select the closest category and add details.

  4. Add evidence
    Screenshots are extremely helpful.

  5. Submit
    Reports are anonymous to the person reported.

What happens after a report

  1. Our Trust & Safety team reviews the report (usually within 24–48 hours).
  2. We may review the user's other listings, chat logs and order history.
  3. Actions range from a warning to listing/temporary removal or permanent suspension.
  4. You'll get an outcome notice where the report allows it.

False reporting

Deliberately false reports harm real users. Repeat false reporting may result in restrictions on your own account.

Emergency or safety-critical reports

If something is unsafe, call local emergency services first, then report in-app and email safety@seli.com.ng with your report ID.

Anonymous by design

Reporters stay anonymous. Users can't see who reported them, and our team only shares evidence needed for an investigation.
