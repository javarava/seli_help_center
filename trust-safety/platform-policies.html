Pilot-specific terms

Pilots operate under the Pilot Service Agreement within the Terms of Service. It covers onboarding, job rules, code of conduct, delivery standards and the requirement to record proof of delivery.

Policy changes

We'll notify you of material policy changes by email and in-app with a summary of what changed. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the updated terms.

Questions about the policies

Contact legal@seli.com.ng, or use the in-app support chat. Arbitration and governing law are set out in the Terms of Service.

Policy vs. help article

Help articles explain how to use Seli; the policies are the binding rules. Where they differ, the policies win.
