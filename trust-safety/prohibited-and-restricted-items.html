Prohibited and Restricted Items

Last updated September 2026

Some items can never be sold on Seli; others may be listed only with approval. Selling a prohibited item leads to removal and account suspension.

Never allowed

CategoryExamples
Illegal goodsDrugs and drug paraphernalia, firearms, ammunition, knives designed as weapons
Fraud & identityFake currency, government IDs, passports, licences, diplomas
Stolen goodsAnything you don't own or have clear rights to sell
HazardousExplosives, flammable chemicals, radioactive materials
Tobacco & alcohol (some)Cigarettes, vapes, hard liquor in most cases
Medicines (some)Prescription-only drugs, unregistered or fake pharmaceuticals
Adult contentPornography, sexual services, used underwear
Personal safetyHuman remains, body parts, weapons attachments

Restricted (approval required)

  • New electronics above a value threshold — may require proof of purchase.
  • Firearms accessories, pepper spray — evaluated case by case.
  • Age-restricted items (e.g., some supplements) — need a verified adult account.
  • Brand-new items being sold under a named brand — may need proof of authorisation.

How approval works

When you list a restricted item, we review it before it goes live. You'll receive a decision by message. If it's declined, we'll say why and you're welcome to remove or amend the listing.

If you're not sure

When in doubt, ask. Start your listing and pick 'Not sure if this is allowed' — we'll check it before it's visible. Zero-risk option: email support@seli.com.ng first.

Consequences of violations

Prohibited listings are removed immediately. Serious or repeated violations lead to suspension, and we co-operate with law enforcement where required by law.

Mistakes happen on purpose too

Listing a product under a false name to bypass these rules (e.g., calling a vape a 'diffuser') is a policy violation. If you see it, report it.
