What is Shemi AI?

Last updated September 2026

Shemi AI is Seli's listing assistant. Upload a photo of any product and Shemi drafts a professional listing for you - title, description and a suggested price - in seconds.

What Shemi can do

  • Turn one photo into a complete listing with a catchy title and description.
  • Suggest a realistic selling price based on the item's category and condition.
  • Rewrite your description to sound more professional and search-friendly.
  • Help you refine listings with follow-up instructions in plain language.

Who is Shemi for?

Shemi is built for sellers - merchants, creators, and anyone with items to sell. You don't need copywriting skills or professional photography. If you can take a clear photo, Shemi handles the rest.

Where to find Shemi

Shemi lives inside the Seli app in the same flow as Store. You can also open it from the Sell tab on the Seli website. After checking what Shemi produces, you stay in control: you review, edit and publish - or discard.

Shemi is a helper, not a replacement

You stay in charge of every listing Shemi creates. Shemi suggests, you decide - and you can always start a listing manually instead.
