Publishing Shemi Listings to Market

Last updated September 2026

Once you're happy with a Shemi draft, publishing it to Seli Market is one tap.

How to publish

  1. Review the draft
    Make sure photos and details are correct, as per Editing and Pricing.

  2. Tap Publish
    Select 'Publish to Market' at the bottom of the draft.

  3. Pick your store
    If you have more than one store, choose which one the listing belongs to.

  4. Done
    The listing goes live in your store. Buyers see it in search and the feed instantly.

Before publishing — a quick checklist

  • First photo is your cover — make it the best shot.
  • Title mentions brand/size/colour.
  • Price includes your delivery decision.
  • Condition stated for used items.
  • No prohibited or restricted content.

Where published listings appear

Published Shemi listings behave exactly like manual ones: they appear in your store, Seli search, the marketplace feed, and category filters. All escrow and delivery protections apply the same way.

Saving as a draft

Not ready to publish? Nothing is lost — drafts save automatically and you can come back from Store → Drafts at any time.

First listing?

If your store isn't verified yet, publish is enabled and your first listings may be held for a quick review before buyers can see them.
