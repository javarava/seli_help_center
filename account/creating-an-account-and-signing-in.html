Creating an Account and Signing In

Last updated September 2026

One Seli account powers everything: Market purchases, Merchant store, Pilot deliveries and Shemi AI.

How to create an account

  1. Choose your method
    Sign up with a phone number or email address.

  2. Verify your phone/email
    We send a one-time code. Enter it to confirm the contact is yours.

  3. Set a password
    Choose a strong password of at least 8 characters.

  4. Add your name
    Tell us the name you'd like to use on Seli.

  5. Done
    You're signed in and ready to browse, buy and sell.

Signing in

  • Open the app and enter your phone/email and password.
  • Prefer no password? Use the one-time code option — we text/email a code that signs you in.
  • You can stay signed in across sessions; sign out from Account → Settings.

Using the same account everywhere

Your Seli account works across the mobile app and the Seli website. Changes (like a new password or notification settings) sync instantly.

Required information

At a minimum we need a verifiable phone number or email, your name, and a password. Additional details — date of birth, address — are needed only when you buy, sell with escrow or deliver.

Deleting your account later

You can remove your account at any time. See Deleting or Deactivating Your Account for what happens to your data and history.

Use a code instead of a password

If you hate remembering passwords, sign in with a one-time code every time — it's just as secure when you keep SMS/email access safe.
