Resetting Your Password

Last updated September 2026

Forgot your password? Recovery takes about a minute when you have access to your phone or email.

Steps to reset

  1. Go to sign in
    Tap 'Forgot password?' on the sign-in screen.

  2. Enter your contact
    Type the phone number or email tied to your account.

  3. Receive the code
    We send a one-time code by SMS or email.

  4. Enter the code
    A new password screen opens once it's correct.

  5. Choose a new password
    At least 8 characters — ideally with a mix of letters, numbers and symbols.

  6. Sign in
    Use your new password immediately.

If you don't receive the code

  • Check your spam folder (for email).
  • Wait 60 seconds and resend — codes refresh.
  • Confirm the number/email you typed matches your account.
  • If it still fails, use support chat — we can verify you by ID.

Password advice

  • Don't reuse passwords from other accounts.
  • Use a passphrase (e.g., 'BlueDog-Banana-2026!').
  • Never share your password — Seli never asks for it.

Updating your password mid-session

Already signed in? Go to Account → Settings → Password to change it from inside the app without the reset flow.

Right after a breach

If you think your account was compromised, reset your password immediately and check Two-Factor Authentication.
