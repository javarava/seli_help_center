Glossary

Last updated September 2026

Plain-language definitions of the words you'll see across Seli.

A – M

TermDefinition
Buyer ProtectionThe guarantee that escrow keeps your payment safe until you confirm delivery
Delivery codeA 4-digit code the buyer gives the Pilot to confirm receipt at drop-off
EscrowA protected holding account that keeps buyer funds until the order completes
Escrow releaseThe automatic release of buyer funds to the seller's wallet, 24 hours after delivery confirmation if no dispute is raised
Job (Pilot)A single delivery request offered to nearby Pilots
ListingA product page a seller publishes, with photos, video, price and description
Merchant / SellerA verified account that owns a store and sells products

N – S

TermDefinition
OnboardingThe application and training steps a new Pilot completes before going live
PayoutYour earnings sent to your linked bank account
Proof of deliveryThe delivery code or handover photo recorded on completion
Protection windowThe 24-hour period after delivery confirmation in which a buyer can raise a claim before escrow auto-releases
RatingA rolling 1–5 average buyers give each Pilot delivery
Seller feeThe 5.5% Seli charge on each completed order, deducted from the payout
ShemiSeli's AI listing assistant that drafts listings from one photo

T – Z

TermDefinition
Trust & SafetyThe team and rules that keep Seli safe — reports, bans and policy enforcement
Verified badgeA mark showing an account passed extra identity and quality checks
Wallet / CreditsYour in-app balance used to pay for orders instantly
