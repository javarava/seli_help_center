FAQ

Last updated September 2026

The quick answers to the questions we hear most from buyers, sellers and pilots.

Buying

  1. Is it safe to pay before I receive my order?
    Yes. Your payment sits in escrow and, if no dispute is raised, is automatically released to the seller 24 hours after delivery confirmation. If the order never arrives, you're refunded — see How Escrow Works.

  2. How long does delivery take?
    Most intra-city deliveries arrive the same day or next day. Exact times show on your order once a Pilot is matched.

  3. The seller wants me to pay outside Seli. Should I?
    Never. Payments made outside the app are not protected by escrow. Please report the seller.

  4. Can I return an item?
    Yes, within 24 hours of delivery confirmation — the protection window on your order. Start with Refunds and Claims.

Selling

  1. How much does it cost to sell on Seli?
    Listing and opening a store are free. There is a 5.5% seller fee on each completed order.

  2. When do I get paid?
    Escrow auto-releases 24 hours after delivery confirmation if no dispute is raised; your bank payout usually lands within 1–2 business days of release.

  3. How do I get more orders?
    Publish clear listings, add video, respond to buyers quickly, and apply for the Verified Seller badge.

  4. What if a buyer damages my item and claims otherwise?
    Evidence decides: photos, video and chat logs are reviewed by our Trust & Safety team before any refund.

Delivery (Pilots)

  1. How do I become a Pilot?
    Apply in the app from the Pilot section, verify your ID, complete onboarding, and go live — full guide here.

  2. How much do Pilots earn?
    You earn a fare per delivery calculated by distance and size, and 100% of the fare is yours.

  3. What if the buyer isn't there at drop-off?
    Wait out the in-app window, keep trying their phone, then follow the return-item flow. Never abandon the order.

  4. Do I need my own bike?
    Motorcycle Pilots need a roadworthy bike and a valid rider's licence. On-foot and bicycle deliveries are available in some areas.

Shemi AI

  1. Is Shemi free?
    Yes, Shemi listing generation is included with your Seli account. There are no charges for drafting listings.

  2. Can Shemi price my item?
    Shemi suggests a price from the item's category and condition. You can accept, edit or override it.

  3. Does Shemi publish without my approval?
    No. Nothing goes live until you review and tap publish.

Account and general

  1. Can I use one account for Market, Pilot and Shemi?
    Yes. One Seli account covers buying, selling, delivering and listing generation.

  2. How do I close my account?
    Deactivate temporarily or delete permanently — both available under Account → Settings.

  3. Where are your policies?
    See Seli Platform Policies for Terms, Privacy, Escrow and Cookie documents.
