Using Your Credits

Last updated September 2026

Your Shop-to-Earn credits behave like money on Seli: you can check them any time, and apply them toward future orders at checkout.

Checking your balance

Open Profile → Shop-to-Earn Balance to see your wallet at a glance and a full list of transactions. Your wallet shows three numbers:

  • Balance - credits available to spend right now.
  • Pending - credits earned but not yet released (their related order has not completed).
  • Total earned - everything earned over time, including credited and spent amounts.

Each transaction shows its description, such as the order that earned it or the checkout that spent it.

Applying credits at checkout

  1. Review your order
    Choose use credits on the payment step. Your available balance is shown.

  2. Choose how much to apply
    Credits can cover part or all of your order total.

  3. Pay the remainder
    If credits don't cover the full total, the remaining amount is paid with your chosen payment method. If they cover the full total, the order is paid with credits alone - no card needed.

You can turn credits off at any time before placing the order if you prefer to pay another way.

Refunds and cancellations

  • If a qualifying order is refunded after credits were released, the credit balance is reversed.
  • If an order is cancelled while a credit is still pending, the pending credit is cancelled.
  • Credits used to pay for an order are handled by the order's normal refund rules - you do not keep credit for cancelled paid items.
Credits are not withdrawable

Shop-to-Earn credits can only be spent on orders inside Seli. They cannot be converted to cash, transferred between accounts, or moved to a bank account.
