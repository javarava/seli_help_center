Earning Credits

Last updated September 2026

There are two ways build up your Shop-to-Earn balance: buying eligible products, and sharing reviews that lead to purchases.

1. Cash back on eligible purchases

When a product is enrolled in Shop-to-Earn, it shows a credit rate - typically 5% of the item price. The merchant chooses the rate and can cap how many credits the promotion gives out.

  1. Check for the indicator
    On the product page you'll see the Shop-to-Earn badge and the credit rate for that item. Your estimated credits for the whole basket are shown at checkout.

  2. Place your order
    The eligible credit is calculated from the paid item price (after any discounts, before delivery fees) and recorded as pending.

  3. Receive your credits
    Once the order is delivered and confirmed with no claim raised, the credit is released into your available balance.

A product may stop earning when its promotion ends (an expiry date set by the merchant) or when the merchant's credit cap for that product is reached. Unenrolled products do not earn credits.

2. Referral credits from your reviews

After a product is delivered to you, you can post a video or photo review instead of a text-only one. The review appears in the Seli reels feed, and when another buyer purchases the product through your review, you earn a referral credit for that order.

  1. Post a video or photo review
    On a delivered, eligible product, choose the earning review option and submit your media review.

  2. Buyers discover it
    Your review appears in discovery feeds with your product linked. Open it and shop normally.

  3. You earn per qualifying purchase
    Each order bought through your review credits your wallet, pending release until that order completes.

To post an earning review you must have received the product within the review window (usually within two months of delivery) with no existing active review for it. You cannot earn a referral from a purchase you make yourself.

Reviews are for real experiences

Share reviews only for products you actually ordered and received, and never ask friends to buy through your review to inflate earnings. Abuse can lead to cancelled credits or account limits. See Reporting a User or Listing.
