What is Shop-to-Earn?

Last updated September 2026

Shop-to-Earn is Seli Market's cash-back rewards programme. When you buy eligible products, you earn credits that work like money on Seli - you can use them to pay for future orders, just as you would spend cash.

Every eligible product shows a Shop-to-Earn indicator with a credit rate, usually 5% of the purchase price. There are two ways to earn:

  • Buy eligible products. Complete a qualifying purchase and the credit amount lands in your wallet.
  • Post video or photo reviews. After a delivered order, share a review. When another buyer later purchases the product through your review, you earn a referral credit for each qualifying bought order.

How credits work

Credits are held in your Shop-to-Earn wallet, which you can open any time from your profile. Each credit is worth its value in the same currency as the order (for example, ₦100 of credits is worth ₦100 off a future order).

  1. You make a qualifying purchase
    The eligible credit is recorded as pending on the order.

  2. The order completes
    Once the order is delivered and confirmed with no claim raised, the credit is released into your available balance.

  3. You spend your balance
    At checkout you apply your available credits toward the order total - part or all of it.

What credits are not

  • Credits are not cash you can withdraw. They can only be used to pay for orders on Seli.
  • You cannot earn a referral on your own purchases - buying something through your own review does not earn you another credit.
  • If a qualifying order is refunded or cancelled, the related credits are cancelled or reversed from your wallet.
Where to go next

See Earning Credits for the full rules on qualifying purchases and reviews, and Using Your Credits to manage your wallet and apply credits at checkout.
