Shop-to-Earn for Merchants

Last updated September 2026

Shop-to-Earn lets you reward your buyers and grow word-of-mouth. When you enable it on a product, eligible buyers earn credits on their purchases, and reviewers can earn more when shoppers buy through their reviews of your product.

Enabling Shop-to-Earn on a product

Open Add Product or Edit Product, scroll to the Shop-to-Earn section, and turn the switch on. New products are enrolled by default unless you turn it off.

When the toggle is on, you set:

  • Credit % - the percentage of the item price a buyer earns back. If left on the default (5%), the platform default applies.
  • Max credits - an optional cap on the total credits the promotion gives out for that product. With no cap, the promotion is unlimited and runs until you end it.
  • Promotion expiry - an optional end date. After it, the product stops earning until you extend it.

You'll see a live preview of the "per purchase" credit your buyers will earn as you adjust these settings.

How credits settle

Credits granted on an order are recorded on that order and deducted from the order's escrow amount at settlement, alongside Seli's selling fee and any processing fee. The merchant product page shows, for each enrolled product, the credit percentage, the per-purchase amount, how many credits the promotion has already awarded, and when it ends.

Why enable it?

Buyers can see that your item earns credits, and buying through a reviewed product keeps your listing in front of shoppers. It's a low-cost way to encourage repeat purchases and referee traffic to your store.
