Fees and Pricing

Last updated September 2026

Seli is free to browse and free to list. Revenue comes from a single, transparent seller fee.

What costs what

FeeAmountWho pays
Listing & storeFreeSellers
Seller fee per completed order5.5% of order valueSellers
Buyer feeNone—
Delivery fareVaries by distance/sizeBuyer (or seller if they offer free delivery)
Pilot payout100% of farePilot

Why the 5.5% matters to you

The fee funds the parts of Seli that protect you: escrow custody, verified pilots, dispute resolution, fraud checks and product trust. There are no hidden markups on your listings.

How the fee is calculated

Fee = 5.5% of the item price (delivery fees are excluded). The remaining amount is your payout once the order completes.

Example: item sold for ₦20,000 → fee ₦1,100 → payout ₦18,900, minus any delivery fee you opted to pay.

Fee refunds

If a full refund is approved, the seller fee for that order is credited back to you automatically. On partial refunds, the fee is adjusted to the final paid amount.

Displaying prices to buyers

Buyers see the item price plus a separate delivery fee line at checkout — always transparent. As a seller, you choose whether to offer free delivery (folded into your price) or charge one.

Keep the maths simple

Price your items as a round number your buyer sees at checkout; the 5.5% affects only your payout, not what buyers pay for the product itself.
