Paying on Seli

Last updated September 2026

Seli accepts the payment methods Nigerians actually use, all processed securely through Paystack.

Supported payment methods

  • Debit & credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve) — processed securely by Paystack.
  • Seli credits — top up a balance and pay instantly without card details each time.
  • Bank transfer (where enabled) — an account number is generated for your order.

How to pay

  1. Checkout
    Confirm your order and tap 'Pay'.

  2. Choose a method
    Pick a card, credits, or transfer.

  3. Complete the payment
    Follow the prompts. Card and transfer charges are handled by Paystack.

  4. Protected
    The moment payment succeeds, your order is placed and your funds are in escrow.

Seli credits

You can add money to your Seli balance from Store → Wallet. Credits pay for orders instantly, refund quickly, and never expire as long as your account is active.

Why Paystack?

Paystack is one of Nigeria's most trusted payment gateways. It encrypts your card details and handles PCI-DSS compliance, so Seli never stores your full card number.

Payment troubleshooting

  • Card declined? Try another card or check your card's online-payment limit with your bank.
  • Double charge? One charge will usually reverse automatically within 24 hours; refunds pending longer than that should be raised with us.
  • Transfer not confirming? Transfers can take minutes to hours. The order stays open until the payment arrives.
You'll always get a receipt

Every payment creates a receipt you can download from the order. See Invoices and Receipts.
