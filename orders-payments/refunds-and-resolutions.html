Refunds and Resolutions

Last updated September 2026

When a claim is raised, escrow keeps the funds safe while both sides present their side. Here are the possible outcomes.

Possible resolutions

OutcomeWhen it happensResult
Full refundItem never delivered, wrong item, or broken beyond useBuyer receives the full order value back
Return & refundBuyer changes mind after delivery within the window (seller consent needed sometimes) or item is unsuitableBuyer returns the item; refund issued after return
Partial refundItem delivered but with minor damage or a missing accessoryBuyer keeps the item, seller is paid the remainder
ReplacementSeller agrees to resend a damaged/wrong itemReplacement ships, escrow holds until the new item is delivered
Claim rejectedEvidence shows the order was correct and properly deliveredFunds release to the seller

How a refund is processed

  • Claim approved.

  • Escrow refunds the order value to your original payment method.

  • Card refunds reach your bank in 3–10 business days.

  • Credit refunds are instant.

Partial refunds explained

A partial refund means you keep the item and pay a fair portion. Example: a laptop arrives with a scratch. You keep the laptop, the buyer is refunded ₦5,000, and the seller receives the balance.

Time limits

  • Claims can only be raised within 24 hours of delivery confirmation — the protection window on your order. Escrow auto-releases to the seller if no dispute is raised in that window.
  • Both sides typically have a few days to respond before Seli decides.
  • Appeals are possible within 7 days of a decision.

Disputes and chargebacks

If you paid by card and your bank reverses the charge (a chargeback), we investigate using the order records. Chargebacks on legitimate, delivered orders can lead to account restrictions.

Keep the item while a return is pending

Don't donate, resell or dispose of an item under an approved return. You'll be asked to return it before the refund completes.
