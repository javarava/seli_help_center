Invoices and Receipts

Last updated September 2026

Every financial event on Seli — a purchase, a payout, a refund — comes with a receipt you can download and keep.

Kinds of receipts

  • Purchase receipts — itemised: product price, delivery, discounts, total, payment method and order ID.
  • Refund receipts — for approved refunds, showing the refund amount and date.
  • Payout receipts — for merchants and pilots, showing the gross order value, fees and amount paid.

Where to find them

  1. Buyers
    Open the order and tap 'Receipt' — available on every completed or refunded order.

  2. Merchants
    Store → Earnings → tap the payout or order line → 'Receipt'.

  3. Pilots
    Pilot dashboard → Earnings → tap any completed delivery → 'Receipt'.

Receipt contents

Receipts include Seli's business details, your email, the transaction reference, an itemised breakdown, and the date. They're formatted for bookkeeping and tax purposes.

Emailing a receipt

On the receipt screen, tap 'Email receipt' to send a PDF to the email address on your account. PDFs are generated on demand and arrive within minutes.

Tax note

Seli receipts reflect amounts paid to or by you, net of any fees. For guidance on VAT or income tax on online trade, please consult a registered accountant.
