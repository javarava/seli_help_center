About Seli Pilot

Last updated September 2026

Seli Pilot is the delivery arm of Seli. Pilots are verified independent riders who collect items from sellers and deliver them to buyers within a city — turning on-demand courier work into a steady additional income.

What Pilots do

  • Pick up items from merchants, sellers or shopping malls.
  • Deliver orders to buyers with delivery codes and proof of delivery.
  • Broadcast deliveries efficiently using live location and the delivery line.

Who can become a Pilot

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You need a working smartphone and an active mobile number.
  • You must have a valid means of identification (NIN, driver's licence or passport).
  • For deliveries by motorcycle, you need a valid rider's licence and your own bike in good condition; deliveries on foot or by bicycle are also possible in some areas.

How Pilots earn

Pilots are paid per delivery. Fares depend on distance, item size and urgency, and 100% of the fare goes to the Pilot — Seli does not take a cut from the fare. Detailed breakdown: Pilot Earnings and Payouts.

Pilot vs. courier apps

Unlike general courier apps, Seli Pilot is deeply integrated with escrow: you collect only after payment is secured, and you always record proof of delivery — so you're never chasing an unpaid customer.
