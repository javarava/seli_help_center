Pilot Safety and Support

Last updated September 2026

Your safety comes first. Here's how to protect yourself on the road and where to go when you need help.

Safety on the road

  • Wear a helmet whenever you ride — no exceptions.
  • Keep to speed limits and watch for large vehicles; most delivery collisions happen at junctions.
  • Secure your phone on a proper mount so you're not distracted while riding.
  • Carry items in a closed bag or box so nothing is visible to thieves.
  • Trust your instincts — if a pickup location seems unsafe, skip the job.

At pickup and drop-off

  • Share your live location with a trusted contact as you ride to risky areas.
  • For high-value orders, agree an inside-lobby handover instead of the open street.
  • Never accept requests to 'just leave it and go' without recording proof in the app.

Getting help from Seli

  • Support chat — in the Pilot dashboard for delivery-specific issues.
  • pilots@seli.com.ng — for applications, earnings and account questions.
  • Emergency — for an urgent on-road situation, notify in-app and call the local emergency number first.

Reporting a safety concern

If a buyer or seller behaves threateningly, end the delivery safely, record what happened in the app, and report the person through Reporting a User. We review reports and take action without exposing your details.

Don't deliver to unverified addresses alone

If a drop-off address looks empty or unverified and the buyer won't confirm their identity, follow the 'confirm without code' flow and contact support before finalising.
